# 整除分块

## 向下取整的整除分块

$$
r=\left\lfloor\frac{n}{⌊\frac{n}{l}⌋}\right\rfloor
$$

## 向上取整的整除分块

$$
\left\lceil \frac{n}{x} \right\rceil = \left\lfloor \frac{n-1}{x} \right\rfloor +1	\\
r=\left\lfloor \frac{n-1}{\lfloor\frac{n+l-1}{l}-1⌋}\right\rfloor = \left\lfloor\frac{n-1}{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{l}\rfloor}\right\rfloor
$$
